# Stratum — Evidence-First Document Intelligence for CMPDI / CIL

*SIH 2026 solution plan · revision 2 (reviewed and redesigned).
The first draft is preserved at [`docs/plan-v1-original.md`](docs/plan-v1-original.md).*

## Exact SIH Problem Statement (Verbatim)

**Background:**

CMPDI/CIL subsidiaries play a key role in providing geological and mining information to the Ministry of Coal and responding to parliamentary and high-priority administrative inquiries. These reports require compilation of data from scanned PDFs, digital documents, spreadsheets, images, and historical archives. The current workflow is largely manual, resulting in:

• High dependence on individual expertise
• Delay in generating reports and analytics
• Higher probability of manual errors
• Limited ability to quickly retrieve insights when required

**Objectives:**
• Deploy an automated platform for AI-assisted geological, mining and any other production figures document processing and reporting.
• Enhance data validation, consistency, and traceability across historical and contemporary datasets.
• Build an efficient, scalable foundation for future digital transformation initiatives within each CIL subsidiary and the Ministry of Coal.

**Desired Outcomes:** The solution should be implemented in structured phases, including requirement analysis, data digitization and pre-processing, platform development, system testing, integration with CIL subsidiary workflows, training, and continuous enhancement to ensure scalability and long-term adoption.

1. Automated Report Generation Platform 2. Automated Word Cloud and Topic Identification Module 3. AI-Based Query and Response System

**Expected Benefits:**

• Reduction in report preparation time as less as it can be, quantified in percentage.
• Maximum accuracy, calculated in percentage in structured extraction and report generation.
• Maximum automation, calculated in percentage of repetitive reporting and response workflows.
• Faster response to high-level inquiries and parliamentary questions
• Improved data accessibility, transparency, and standardization
• Strengthened operational efficiency and informed decision-making using historical insights and AI-generated recommendations

**Impact:**

The proposed system should significantly modernize CMPDI/CIL subsidiaries reporting ecosystem, reduce dependency on manual processes, improve response timelines, and strengthen the coal sector's capability to support governance, policy planning, and operational excellence.

## 1. The idea in one paragraph

Stratum is a **local-first platform that turns CMPDI/CIL documents into verified facts and cited answers.**
Scanned reports, digital PDFs, spreadsheets and images are parsed into one evidence format. Every table
cell keeps its page and its position on the page. Production figures are pulled out into a fact database,
and **the numbers are copied from the cells, never generated by the AI**. Deterministic checks validate
them, and a reviewer confirms anything that fails. Questions, parliamentary replies and reports are
answered from those facts plus the surrounding text, with **a citation on every number** that opens the
exact source cell. Everything runs on one machine with open-weight models. Nothing leaves it, and the UI
shows that.

The name: *strata* are the layers of a coal-bearing formation. Stratum is layered the same way:
original file, then page, then cell, then verified fact, then answer.

## 2. What we changed after reviewing revision 1, and why

Revision 1 had the right instincts: numbers live in a database, provenance comes first, accuracy is
measured. But it could not be built or demonstrated on realistic hardware, and it left the hardest problem
as a single bullet. What changed:

| # | Revision 1 | Problem found in review | Revision 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fork RAGFlow as the shell | Needs 16 GB RAM by itself (Elasticsearch/Infinity, MySQL, MinIO, Kvrocks, NATS, ClickHouse). Its MySQL becomes a second source of truth. Citations are chunk-level, not cell-level. It is a fast-moving release candidate. | Dropped. Our own hybrid search (vector + keyword, reciprocal-rank fusion) in about 300 lines, over one database. |
| 2 | Laya models for routing and validation | Laya's own README reports near-chance zero-shot accuracy on typed decisions (0.362, vs 0.766 fine-tuned), and we have no labelled routing data yet. Validating a number is arithmetic, not classification. | Deterministic rules first. For query intent, a local LLM whose output is **forced to match a JSON schema**. Validation is deterministic. A fine-tuned Laya/SetFit router is future work once real query logs exist. |
| 3 | Auto-accept at confidence ≥ 0.98 | No parser emits calibrated per-fact confidence, and OCR confidence is not number correctness. | **Evidence-based status**: `verified` (two independent sources agree, or a human approved), `consistent` (all checks pass), `flagged` (a named check failed). |
| 4 | "Production fact extraction" as one bullet | This is most of the real work: multi-level headers, "Total CIL" rows, "*provisional" footnotes, units hidden in captions, FY notation variants. | **"The LLM maps, the code extracts."** The model only says which column is which metric, period and unit, and which row is which subsidiary. Code copies the digits verbatim from the cells, so the model *cannot* invent a value. |
| 5 | Free-form natural language → SQL | A small model writing raw SQL is unreliable and unsafe. | **Semantic layer**: the model fills a `QueryIntent` (metric, entities, periods, grouping, comparison), and our compiler turns it into parameterized SQL. |
| 6 | Graph database route | Mine → subsidiary → coalfield → state is master data. | Relational master tables. The UI can still draw the relationships. |
| 7 | ColPali visual retrieval, PageIndex | Neither fits beside the LLM on a 4 GB GPU, and PageIndex needs a strong reasoning model. | The vision model reads figures and badly scanned pages **at ingest time**, and its description becomes searchable text. Every chunk keeps its section heading path. |
| 8 | Docling **and** MinerU | Two heavy parsers to integrate. MinerU's GPU tiers need 8 GB+ VRAM. | Docling (OCR only on pages with no text layer) plus the vision fallback. MinerU is the scale-up path. |
| 9 | PQ reply = "short answer" | Real Lok Sabha / Rajya Sabha replies are structured: parts (a), (b), (c) and annexure tables. The ministry's real risk is **contradicting an earlier reply to Parliament**. | **PQ Reply Builder**: split the parts, answer each with citations, build annexures, find similar past replies, warn on inconsistencies, export official-format DOCX. |
| 10 | No provisional vs final figures | Monthly figures are provisional, and the Coal Directory is final. The same metric legitimately differs across sources. | `is_provisional` flag, **source precedence**, and a discrepancy callout that shows both values. |
| 11 | "The LLM should never invent values", as a hope | Nothing enforced it. | **Number guard**: every number in generated text must match the evidence bundle, or it is flagged on screen. This also gives the *unsupported-claim rate* metric. |
| 12 | 500-fact gold set before any data exists | Unrealistic. | A small, honest gold set first (about 100 facts, 40 questions, timed PQ trials), grown with real data. |
| 13 | Topic model over 20–50 documents | Too few documents give noise topics. | Topics over thousands of chunks plus past parliamentary questions by year: what Parliament asks about coal, over time. |
| 14 | English only | Parliamentary material is bilingual. | Multilingual embeddings. Hindi questions are answered in Hindi. |
| 15 | Postgres + MinIO + Celery + Redis + Next.js | Too many services for one demo machine. | SQLite (FTS5 keyword search + vector search) in one file, a content-addressed file store, and the DeepSeek-Harness web client as the UI. PostgreSQL + pgvector is the production target behind the same SQL layer. |
| 16 | "Local-first" vs a public demo link | Not addressed. | One model interface. Local inference by default, and a clearly **disclosed** replay mode for a hosted walkthrough. |

### Components (MVP)

| Layer | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| UI | DeepSeek Harness web client + our out-of-tree plugin (forked from our Faraday workbench) | A production-grade agent UI: sessions, streaming, tool cards, deliverables. We add panels in declared slots, not a new frontend. |
| Parsing | Docling (MIT), pypdfium2 page rendering | Layout, reading order, TableFormer tables, page + bbox provenance. Runs on CPU. |
| Vision | Qwen3-VL-2B (Apache-2.0), local | Figures, maps, bad scans. Only on selected pages. |
| Language model | Qwen3-4B (Apache-2.0), local via llama.cpp + llama-swap | Fits a 4 GB GPU. Schema-constrained JSON output. |
| Embeddings | multilingual-e5-small (MIT), CPU | English + Hindi, fast on CPU. |
| Store | SQLite + FTS5 + vector index (MVP) → PostgreSQL + pgvector (production) | One file for the demo; same SQL layer later. |
| Backend | Python, FastAPI | Ingestion, facts, search, ask, PQ, reports, topics, metrics. |
| Topics | BERTopic + KeyBERT/YAKE, word cloud | Topic-over-time and drill-down, not a cosmetic cloud. |
| Documents out | python-docx, matplotlib | Official-format DOCX with charts and citations. |

### The data model (core tables)

`documents` (sha256, filename, type, subsidiary, year, source kind, precedence) · `pages` · `blocks` (text,
heading path, bbox) · `tables` / `cells` (row, col, text, bbox) · `chunks` (+ vectors, FTS) · `facts`
(entity, metric, value, unit, period, provisional, status) · `fact_sources` (fact ↔ cell lineage, raw
text, unit conversion) · `fact_checks` (check, result, detail) · master data (`subsidiaries`,
`coalfields`, `states`, `metrics`, `units`, `aliases`) · `pq_archive` · `answers` / `citations` ·
`reviews` · `audit_log`.

## 4. The three required modules

### 4.1 AI-Based Query and Response System
- The router classifies each request, and the chip under the composer shows the route and why.
- **Numerical questions** go to the SQL route: `QueryIntent` → compiler → facts, each with its lineage.
- **Narrative questions** go to hybrid retrieval (vector + keyword, fused), with cited passages.
- **Mixed questions** ("why did production fall in FY2019-20?") combine the trend table with the documents that explain it.
- Every answer carries its citations, verification status, period and unit, and a discrepancy warning when sources disagree.
- With no evidence, the answer is: *"Insufficient verified evidence available."*

### 4.2 PQ Reply Builder (parliamentary and ministry requests)
1. Paste the question: Starred/Unstarred, Lok Sabha/Rajya Sabha, parts (a)(b)(c).
2. Each part is answered separately, from facts and cited evidence.
3. Tables become an **Annexure**.
4. **Similar past questions** are retrieved, and figures already given to Parliament are compared with today's. A mismatch is flagged before the reply leaves the building.
5. Export: DOCX in the ministry's reply format, ready for officer review.

### 4.3 Automated Report Generation Platform
- Templates are YAML: required data (as `QueryIntent`s), tables, charts, narrative prompts.
- MVP templates: subsidiary-wise annual production, target vs achievement, multi-year trend.
- Narrative is written only from the numbers in the bundle, and the number guard checks it.
- Output: DOCX with charts, tables and a sources section. Every number is traceable.

### 4.4 Automated Word Cloud and Topic Identification Module
- BERTopic over document chunks and the parliamentary-question archive.
- Topic labels, key phrases, topic frequency **over time** and by subsidiary, drill-down to the source passages.
- The word cloud (domain stop-words removed) is one view of this, not the whole module.

## 5. Validation, provenance, human review

**Checks** run on every extracted fact:
- Row and column totals equal the sum of their parts.
- The % growth column is recomputed.
- The unit is known and consistent.
- The financial year is valid.
- The value is non-negative and within a plausible range for its metric.
- Year-on-year jump is within bounds.
- Cross-source agreement.
- Provisional vs final.
- No duplicates.

**Lineage** kept for every number: file hash → page → table → row/column → raw cell text → normalized
value → unit conversion → parser and version → checks → reviewer.

**Review queue**: flagged facts show the page image with the cell highlighted, the parsed row, the
failed check and its reason. The reviewer accepts, edits or rejects, and every edit is logged. The
queue is the human-in-the-loop, and the rate of corrections is itself a tracked metric.

## 6. How we measure the % the problem statement asks for

| Claim | Metric | How it is computed |
|---|---|---|
| Accuracy of structured extraction | exact-match %, precision / recall / F1 | Extracted facts vs a hand-verified gold set |
| Accuracy of answers and reports | numeric correctness %, citation correctness %, unsupported-number rate | Gold questions with known answers, plus the number guard |
| Automation | automation % = steps completed without human intervention ÷ total repetitive steps | Pipeline step log (ingest, extract, validate, answer, format) |
| Time reduction | (manual time − assisted time) ÷ manual time | Timed trials: the same PQ answered by hand vs with Stratum |
| Routing | routing accuracy % | Labelled question set |

The numbers are computed by a script from the running system and shown on the Metrics tab. None are asserted.

## 7. Security and deployment

- **Air-gapped by construction.** Local models; the seal denies and counts every outbound attempt, on screen.
- Immutable originals (content-addressed), audit log of queries, answers, reports and reviews.
- Roles (viewer / reviewer / admin) and subsidiary-scoped document visibility in the production phase.
- Model-agnostic: approved models are swapped in the registry, and a licence gate refuses non-permissive weights.
- Production: PostgreSQL + pgvector, object storage, SSO, and one GPU server per subsidiary or a central cluster.

## 8. Phased rollout (as the problem statement requires)

| Phase | Scope | Exit criterion |
|---|---|---|
| 0 Requirement analysis | Top 20 recurring reports, top 100 recurring questions, metric/unit vocabulary, access rules, manual baseline timings | Signed-off domain vocabulary and a benchmark specification |
| 1 Digitization & pre-processing | Bulk ingest of archives, OCR on scans, master data, alias tables | Every document searchable, with page provenance |
| 2 Platform development | Facts, validation, ask, PQ builder, reports, topics | MVP modules on real data |
| 3 System testing | Gold sets, adversarial and conflict tests, access-control and load tests | Published accuracy / automation / time metrics |
| 4 Integration | SSO, subsidiary repositories, existing databases, approval workflow | Pilot in one subsidiary + a ministry cell |
| 5 Training | Operators, reviewers, administrators; SOPs | Trained users per subsidiary |
| 6 Continuous enhancement | Review corrections feed extraction rules; router fine-tuning (Laya/SetFit) on logged queries; benchmark growth; model upgrades gated on evaluation | Quarterly quality audit |

## 10. Demo storyline (video, about 3 minutes)

1. **The problem**: a PQ lands at 5 pm, and the figures sit in scanned reports and three spreadsheets.
2. **Ingest**: drop a scanned report; tables become facts, and one fails a total check and goes to review.
3. **Ask**: "subsidiary-wise coal production, FY2022-23 vs FY2023-24" returns an exact table with citations. Click a number and the source cell is highlighted.
4. **Why?**: a mixed question shows the trend plus the documents explaining it.
5. **PQ reply**: paste a real Lok Sabha question and get parts (a)(b)(c), an annexure, a warning that a figure differs from a reply given last session, and a DOCX.
6. **Report**: one click gives a formatted DOCX with charts.
7. **Topics**: what Parliament asked about coal, year by year.
8. **Metrics + seal**: measured percentages, and zero bytes left the machine.

## 11. Differentiators

1. **Numbers are copied, not generated**: the LLM maps tables and code extracts the cells.
2. **Cell-level traceability**: any number opens its page with the cell highlighted.
3. **Consistency with past replies to Parliament**: catches contradictions before they are sent.
4. **Number guard**: generated text cannot state a figure that isn't in the evidence.
5. **Honest metrics**: computed from the running system, shown on screen.
6. **Air-gapped on a laptop GPU**: open-weight models, zero egress, a licence gate on models.

What those numbers do not show, stated plainly:

- They are a small synthetic corpus. Real scans are messier; the OCR clean-up check (a stray "." after "62.01")
  already found one such case and records it on the fact.
- Time reduction is the figure the problem statement cares most about and the one nobody can compute for
  you. The Metrics tab shows "no timed trials yet" until a person is timed doing the same PQ by hand and with
  Stratum; `metrics/gold.yaml` has the field.
- The deployable link is a simulation: the model plane's `replay` provider is disclosed on screen whenever it
  is active. It is decided later, with the demo video.
